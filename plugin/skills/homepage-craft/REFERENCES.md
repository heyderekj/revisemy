# Reference shelf

These are sites worth pointing makers to in homepage feedback, grouped by the problem they help with. When you suggest references, pick two or three that are **distinct from each other**, so the maker sees a range rather than one look to copy. Say what to take from each one in a few words.

Sites change. Open a reference before recommending it, and drop it if it no longer shows what it's listed for.

## Personality and brand identity

For a product with great bones but no personality, where people describe it by what it connects to.

- [buttondown.com](https://buttondown.com) — an email tool with a voice of its own
- [loops.so](https://loops.so) — a crisp, confident product brand in the same category
- [bentonow.com](https://bentonow.com) — a different take again, to show the range

## Dark themes done well

For a dark site that feels heavy or unrefined, or to compare before switching to light.

- [renewedvision.com/propresenter](https://renewedvision.com/propresenter) — a dark product page for church software
- [discord.com](https://discord.com) — dark with color and illustration doing the lifting
- [heyalice.app](https://heyalice.app) — a calm, refined dark product page

## Editorial and handmade brands

For a product whose site could become the brand: type, texture and a point of view.

- [crusheditorial.com](https://crusheditorial.com)
- [cleftnotes.com](https://cleftnotes.com)

## The app icon as the hero

For a simple app where the icon is the strongest asset and the current mockups make visitors work.

- [beeper.com](https://beeper.com)
- [flighty.com](https://flighty.com)
- [tiempo.fitness](https://tiempo.fitness) — a simple app that pushes its color and icon all the way

## A cohesive visual system

For visuals that each look fine but feel random together.

- [butter.video](https://butter.video)
- [ballpark.ing](https://ballpark.ing)

## Feature breakdowns

For a product whose features are a wall of text or a list of checkmarks.

- [worshiptools.com/en-us/charts](https://worshiptools.com/en-us/charts) — features broken down with real views of the app

## Logo polish

- [flockconnect.com](https://flockconnect.com) — a polished mark and wordmark for church software

## Type

- [fontshare.com](https://fontshare.com) — a free library of good typefaces, for replacing an overused display font

## Directories and search-first products

- [businessdirectory-template.webflow.io](https://businessdirectory-template.webflow.io) — search, filters and example listings up front
